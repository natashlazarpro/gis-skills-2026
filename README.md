GIS 2026: a metro map of geospatial skills 🚇

What if the whole geospatial toolbox looked like the Berlin mobility map?

The idea comes from Shashank Dhande Dhande’s GeoAI metro map. The skills come from Matt Forrest Forrest’s modern-GIS writing. I put them together!

The lines: Research, Data Mining, Data Engineering, Statistics, Analysis, Machine Learning, GeoAI, Data Visualisation and Publishing. 

The map has three zones, like fare zones:
A – Junior: core tools and everyday GIS work
B – Middle: workflows, automation and modelling
C – Senior: scale, research and AI engineering

How I read it: pick a line, ride it outwards from zone A, and see how far you’ve already travelled. The line you ride least is probably your next stop.
